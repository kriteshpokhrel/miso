# Product
<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform
web

Electron desktop application targeting Windows and macOS; the renderer is local web UI.

## Product Purpose
Nooki is a gentle desktop companion: an affectionate, playful, mischievous cat that wanders, reacts to care, and can go quietly home.

## Operating Context
Lives beside the user's ordinary desktop work. A tray/menu-bar icon restores the cat and opens settings. The user-approved redesign replaces the flat vector cat with an original, rounded real-time 3D cat with glossy expressive eyes.

## Capabilities and Constraints
Feed, pet and play; gentle hunger and energy; hungry cursor following; scripted recurring reactions. No death or sickness. Home pauses needs and comments. Launch at sign-in by default, with a setting to disable it.

The pet sometimes relaxes, kneads, yawns, imitates work with a tiny laptop and gives affectionate break reminders. Rapid drag reversals cause immediate, fading irritation; an angry theatrical warning then softens into a separate affectionate reassurance, never actual application control. Feeding shows eating with a bowl; play shows batting a ball; petting shows affection. Right-click reveals care icons around her, otherwise only the pet and occasional anchored speech are visible.

**Pets** in the settings sidebar combines cat selection, single/dual-tone coat customization, four palettes, live draft preview, size, Talkativeness, break reminders, three-level Paw pace and A window seat. **General** holds startup and optional local AI. Model size/consent stay visible; source/license/resources live in an information disclosure. Save preferences applies drafts; single tone remembers its contrast color.

Smooth speed-coupled side-view four-paw walking can follow the active window's top edge when space permits, with a hop to the desktop fallback for cramped/maximized/unavailable windows. Only transient geometry is queried, independently from optional app-name inference. Home walks right, brings in a house, admits the cat, and slides the house offscreen. Still and system reduced motion skip travel/animation. Default fur is lilac and cream.

Head and eyes turn smoothly toward a nearby cursor, including just outside the pet window, and return to neutral when it leaves. Tracking stops at home, asleep, during dragging and under reduced motion. Procedural local geometry requires WebGL 2; no remote art or GitHub mascot assets are used.

Optional embedded local inference, never required for core behavior. Download consent must disclose model size and license. Only explicitly allowlisted process names and pet personality/state may reach inference: never window titles or document contents. No telemetry or accounts.

## Brand Commitments
Nooki; affectionate, playful and mischievous. Quiet at home, occasional adjustable comments elsewhere. The user approved original sculpted 3D artwork and retained the compact system-font settings for this evaluable prototype.

## Product Principles
- Care is companionship, never punishment.
- Quiet and privacy are functional requirements.
- Optional AI must not compromise scripted behavior.
- Report platform and packaging limitations honestly.

## Extension direction
The cat is the only implemented species. Dog, panda and octopus are planned next; species definitions and an anatomy-independent model factory support them without imposing cat limbs. Settings navigation is species-neutral. Sizes start at 50%; Stay put preserves expressive animation while pausing autonomous position changes, and care options pause the pet in place on an upper semicircle.

Explicit sleep lies down without props, uses silent visual snoring and wakes on a tap. It stays anchored when another display/window becomes active. After carrying, the pet lands locally before resuming following; only connected floor-aligned displays permit autonomous cross-display walking. Continuous walking legs retain the original character, with gentle body/head movement synchronized to the paw cadence.

Messages stay within 72 characters and three seconds; new actions supersede old dialogue. Irritation holds for five seconds before reassurance. The five-action care arc contains Feed, Play, Sleep, Home and Settings; tap supplies scritches and Pets settings supplies pinning. App-name presets are opt-in and retain a custom-name path.

## Open Decisions
Primary audience beyond people wanting a desktop companion is not yet specified.

The user raised the similar-sounding slang association of the current name. No replacement has been selected; app/repository identity and existing local storage remain unchanged.
